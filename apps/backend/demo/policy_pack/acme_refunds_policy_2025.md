# ACME Widget Corp - Refund Policy (2025)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document ID:** POL-2025-REFUND

## 1. Overview
This document outlines the standard refund and return procedures for all ACME Widget Corp products purchased during the 2025 fiscal year. ACME is committed to customer satisfaction while maintaining operational efficiency.

## 2. Return Eligibility Window
Customers may return eligible items within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
- Items must be in original packaging.
- Proof of purchase is required.
- Requests made after 30 days will be systematically denied.

## 3. Restocking Fees
To cover inspection and repackaging costs, ACME applies a restocking fee to all non-defective returns.
- **Standard Restocking Fee:** **10%** of the item's purchase price.
- This fee is deducted effectively from the refund amount.
- Defective items are exempt from restocking fees if reported within 48 hours.

## 4. Processing & Chargebacks
Once a return is received at our warehouse, please allow up to **7 business days** for the refund to be processed and credited back to the original payment method.

## 5. Exceptions
- Custom-ordered widgets are non-refundable.
- Software licenses are non-refundable once activated.
