# ACME Employee Handbook
**Version:** 4.2
**Department:** Human Resources

## 1. Code of Conduct
All ACME employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for harassment or discrimination.

## 2. Standard Benefits
ACME provides a competitive benefits package including:
- Health, Dental, and Vision insurance (eligible after 30 days).
- 401(k) matching up to 4%.
- Paid Time Off (PTO) accruing at 1.5 days per month.

## 3. Office Kitchen Policy
The kitchen is a shared space. Please clean up after yourself.
- Coffee and tea are provided free of charge.
- The refrigerator is cleared out every Friday at 5:00 PM. Anything left behind will be discarded.

## 4. Security
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Use 2FA for all internal systems.
