# ACME Widget Corp - Updated Refund Policy (2026)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document ID:** POL-2026-REFUND-V2

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the 2026 fiscal year specific policies. We have updated our return terms to better uphold our quality standards and logistics capabilities. This policy supersedes previous iterations.

## 2. Extended Return Window
Effective immediately for all 2026 purchases, the return eligibility window has been extended. Customers now have **45 days** from the date of purchase to initiate a return.
- Items must be unused and in like-new condition.
- A valid receipt or order number is mandatory.

## 3. Updated Restocking Fees
Due to increased logistics and warehousing costs, the standard restocking fee has been adjusted for the 2026 operational year.
- **Restocking Fee:** **15%** of the gross purchase price.
- This increase ensures we can maintain our high standards of quality control for returned goods.

## 4. Refund Processing Times
Due to new accounting compliance measures, the processing time for refunds has changed. Please allow up to **10 business days** for funds to appear on the customer's statement after the return is inspected.

## 5. Digital & Custom Goods
- As with previous years, custom configurations remain final sale.
- Downloadable content cannot be returned.
