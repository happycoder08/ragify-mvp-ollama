========================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
========================================

Q: What should I wear to the office?
A: Business casual. Think: nice jeans/slacks, collared shirt/blouse. No shorts or flip-flops.

Q: Where do I park?
A: Use the employee parking garage (west entrance). Your badge provides access. Spot assignments are first-come, first-served.

Q: Can I bring my pet to work?
A: We are a dog-friendly office! Dogs must be well-behaved and up-to-date on vaccinations. Register your pet with facilities.

Q: How do I expense something?
A: Use Expensify. Upload receipt, categorize expense, submit for manager approval. Reimbursement within 2 weeks.

Q: What if I'm sick?
A: Notify your manager ASAP via Slack/email. No doctor's note needed unless out 3+ consecutive days. Use sick days without guilt.

Q: Can I work from a different city/state temporarily?
A: Yes, with manager approval. Maximum 2 weeks at a time due to tax/compliance reasons. Must maintain regular working hours.

Q: How do I request equipment (monitor, keyboard, etc.)?
A: Submit ticket via IT portal or Slack #it-requests. Most requests approved within 3 business days.
